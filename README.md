# 🌸 Ferns and Petals (FNP) – Sales Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing sales data for Ferns and Petals (FNP), a company specializing in gifting products.

The main objective is to explore sales performance, identify business trends, understand customer purchasing behavior, and generate meaningful insights using Microsoft Excel.

## 🎯 Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales performance.
- Identify the best-performing products and categories.
- Explore monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Analyze sales distribution across different countries and branches.
- Understand customer purchasing patterns.
- Evaluate revenue and cost-related metrics.
- Support business decisions through data-driven insights.
